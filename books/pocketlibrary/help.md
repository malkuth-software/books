A shelf of books to read. Choose one by clicking its spine, or move along the shelf
with the arrow keys and press Enter. Point at a book to read its title and author on a
slip above it. Every book opens where you left it.

To find a book, type part of its title or its author's name in the search field at
the top (Command or Control F, or Search in the capsule). Only the books found stay on
the shelf; Enter opens the first, and Escape brings back the whole library. Accents
and capitals do not matter.

Turn the pages with the arrow keys, Page Up and Page Down, the space bar, the wheel,
or a click on either page. C shows the book's contents; choose a part to go straight
to it. Escape closes the contents, then returns to the library, as Back does. The
capsule has Library, Contents, Previous page and Next page.

The Settings screen has the type size, the typeface (each book as it is set, or every
book in Literata or in EB Garamond), the paper (India, Book, Wove, Laid, Rag,
Parchment, Recycled, Kraft, Night, light ink on dark paper, and Phosphor, green on
black), the paper's texture and the shadow in the gutter.

The shelves hold 746 books, a plank to each kind. Scripture: the Berean Standard
Bible, the Holy Scriptures of the Jewish Publication Society (1917), the deuterocanon of
the World English Bible, 1 Enoch, Josephus, the Kojiki and the Nihongi. Rabbinic writings: the Mishnah, Gorfinkle's
Sayings of the Jewish Fathers, Cohen's Tractate Berakot, the Talmud in four volumes (the
William Davidson edition, with Steinsaltz's explanation), the Midrash Rabbah, the
Targums, Rashi, Ibn Ezra, Ramban and Sforno on the Torah, the Jerusalem Talmud in four
volumes (Guggenheimer) and the fourteen books of Maimonides' Mishneh Torah (Touger).
Church Fathers: the Apostolic Fathers, Origen and Augustine, and the twenty-eight
volumes of the Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers. Reference: Strong's Hebrew and Greek
dictionaries, a concordance of the Berean Standard Bible, STEPBible's brief Hebrew and
Greek lexicons, and Abbott-Smith's Greek lexicon of the New Testament (Hebrew words are
given in Latin letters).

Then Philosophy, History, Society (politics, economics, law and war) and Science, and
Fiction, Science fiction, Fantasy, Drama, Poetry and Children's books. The first four
planks stand in their traditional order; on the others the books stand by author, and an
author's books in the order they were first published. Scroll the bookcase with the
wheel, or go to a plank from the capsule.

Every book is set exactly as its source gives it. Most are in the public domain; a few
are shared by their makers under Creative Commons licences that ask for credit (some also
ask that the book not be sold, and the Pocket Library never is). Books under a share-alike licence
stand together at the end of their shelf with a small green label low on the spine. I,
or About this book in the capsule, shows any open book's edition, licence and credit.
